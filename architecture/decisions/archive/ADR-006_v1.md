# ADR 006 (v1): Gemini 2.5 Model Family and Tiered Escalation Under Free-Tier Quotas (D6)

## Status
Superseded by [archive/ADR-006_v2.md](./ADR-006_v2.md) and active [ADR-006.md](../ADR-006.md) (v3) — superseded upon confirming Gemini 2.5 deprecation (Dec 1, 2026 rerouting) and deciding to offload Stages 5–6 to GitHub Models (`models: read`) for free-tier quota savings and cross-provider reviewer independence.

## Context
Our agentic SDLC workflow invokes LLMs across six stages and bounded retry loops under a strict zero-cost constraint using a Google AI Studio Free-Tier Gemini API key (`google-genai` SDK). Paid-only providers (OpenAI direct billing, Anthropic Claude) violate our \$0 budget, while running a single model across all stages risks exhausting tight free-tier RPD limits or weakening review independence.

## Decision
We will standardize on the Gemini 2.5 family via workflow environment variables: `gemini-2.5-flash` handles Stages 1, 2, 3 (initial 2 attempts), 4, and 5, while `gemini-2.5-pro` handles Stage 3/4 escalation (1 attempt) and Stage 6 (`ReviewerAgent`).

We rejected paid external providers due to the \$0 cap and rejected running `gemini-2.5-pro` on every stage (and rejected 5+5 retry budgets) to stay within free-tier RPD limits.

## Consequences
Positive consequences include a guaranteed \$0 spending cap and simple single-SDK (`google-genai`) integration.

Negative consequences (discovered during pre-implementation verification) include Gemini 2.5 deprecation (`gemini-2.5-pro` restricted and automatic Dec 1, 2026 rerouting to 3.x models with parameter differences) and single-provider quota pressure when Gemini also handles Stages 5 and 6.
