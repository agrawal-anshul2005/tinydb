# ADR 006 (v5): Cross-Family Free-Tier Model Allocation (Gemini 3.x + Open-Weight Gemma 4) (D6)

## Status
Superseded by active [ADR-006.md](../ADR-006.md) (v6) — superseded after Issue #2's run hit a simultaneous `503` demand wave on `gemini-3.7-flash`/`3.6-flash` and a `16,000` free-tier input-TPM `429` on `gemma-4-31b-it` (while `antigravity-preview-latest` returned `400 INVALID_ARGUMENT: only supports Interactions API`); v6 keeps Gemini 3.x + Gemma 4 primary and adds **Groq Free Tier (`llama-3.3-70b-versatile`, `openai/gpt-oss-120b`, `llama-3.1-8b-instant`)** as a cross-provider last-resort failover.

## Context
After Run 4 revealed GitHub Models (`models.github.ai`) was retired, we migrated Stages 5 and 6 to open-weight Gemma 4 (`gemma-4-31b-it` / `gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`) on Google AI Studio.

## Decision
We ran Stages 1–4 on Gemini 3.x (`gemini-3.5-flash-lite` / `gemini-3.5-flash` / `gemini-3.7-flash`) and Stages 5–6 on open-weight Gemma 4, all on Google AI Studio.

## Consequences
Issue #1 completed all 6 stages cleanly (`PR #8`, 3/3 hidden tests passed, `APPROVE` from `gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`), but Issue #2 demonstrated single-provider fragility during simultaneous Google AI Studio `503`/`429` spikes — motivating v6's cross-provider Groq Free Tier last-resort failover.
